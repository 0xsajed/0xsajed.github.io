Models

3D models available in the Rubik's Cube Solver.

3x3 Cube

The 3x3 is the basic and most common Rubik's cube out there. This cube is the logical basis for the rest of the cubes on this website.

Mastermorphix

The Mastermorphix is a shape-shifter cube which might look very daunting despite the fact that it is mathematically equivalent to a 3x3, and can therefore be solved with exactly the same method. It's a rounded tetrahedral shape which is cut into 3x3 pieces.

Mirror cube

The Mirror cube is a cube which was originally marketed as a Rubik's cube for blind people. It is still mathematically equal to a 3x3, but instead of solving based on colours, each pieces can be uniquely identified with its shape. This cube is in a solved state whenever it is actually in a cube shape.
